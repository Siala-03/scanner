# 📱 Online Ordering System - Quick Reference Guide

## 👨‍💼 For Restaurant Managers

### Getting Your QR Code
1. Go to **Settings → Online Ordering** (or Manager Dashboard)
2. Click **"Generate QR Code"** 
3. Your unique QR code is now active

### Share Your QR Code
**Download & Print:**
- Click **"Download QR Code"** button
- Print the image (A4 or smaller)
- Display in your restaurant entrance, on tables, or at counter

**Share Online:**
- Click **"Copy Link"** button
- Paste in your social media posts (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp)
- Share on your website
- Send via email to customers

### Example Posts for Social Media:

**Instagram Caption:**
```
🍽️ Order online and get faster service! 
Scan the QR code or click the link in bio. 
No waiting, just delicious food! 📱
Link: [your-short-link]
#OnlineOrdering #FoodDelivery
```

**WhatsApp Status:**
```
📱 Order online now!
Scan the QR code or visit: [your-short-link]
Fast, easy, and convenient!
```

### What If Something Goes Wrong?
- **Old code not working?** → Click **"Regenerate Code"** (creates new one, old one stops working)
- **Need a new design?** → Download again with fresh design
- **Lost the link?** → Just generate a new code

## 👨‍🍳 For Kitchen Staff

### Where Online Orders Appear
- Kitchen receives orders just like table orders
- Orders marked as **"Online"** so you know it's from QR code
- Order includes customer name and special instructions

### Order Status Workflow
1. **Pending** → Order received, check if ready to prepare
2. **Preparing** → Start cooking/preparing items
3. **Ready** → Place finished order in pickup area
4. **Waiter picks up** → Notifies customer

### What to Look For
- **Special Instructions** → Read any dietary notes
- **Timing** → Customers are waiting, prioritize accordingly
- **Item Count** → Check all items are included

## 🎯 For Supervisors - APPROVAL WORKFLOW

### ⚠️ New Requirement: You Must Approve All Online Orders

**Why?** Prevents kitchen overload and ensures quality control.

### Monitor Online Orders
**Location:** Supervisor Dashboard → **Online Orders** section

**What You See (Status Summary):**
- 🔴 **Awaiting Approval** → Orders need YOUR decision
- 🔵 **Approved** → Orders sent to kitchen
- 🟠 **Preparing** → Kitchen is working
- 🟢 **Ready** → Ready for waiter pickup

### Your Daily Task:

1. **Check Dashboard** - Look for red "⚠️ Awaiting Supervisor Approval" section
2. **Review Order** - Check items, special instructions, total
3. **Click Button:**
   - **✅ Approve** → Sends to kitchen (order starts)
   - **❌ Reject** → Cancels order (customer gets email)
4. **Monitor Progress** - Watch orders move through statuses
5. **Communicate with Kitchen** - Let them know capacity

### When to Reject an Order?
- Items are out of stock
- Kitchen is too busy right now
- Can't fulfill special requests
- Need customer clarification

### Performance Tips
- ⚡ Approve quickly - customers are waiting
- 🔥 During rush: batch approve multiple orders
- 📞 Talk to kitchen about capacity
- 💬 Contact customer if rejecting

## 🚗 For Waiters - New Online Order View

### Where Online Orders Are
**Location:** Waiter Dashboard → **Online Orders** section

**Organized By (Priority Order):**
- 🎯 **Ready for Pickup** (⭐ HIGHEST PRIORITY - customers waiting!)
- 👨‍🍳 **Being Prepared** (informational, check back soon)
- ⏳ **Awaiting Supervisor Approval** (just FYI, you can't do anything)

### Your Tasks

#### **Section 1: 🎯 Ready for Pickup** ← DO THIS FIRST!
```
1. Check this section first - customers waiting!
2. Gather all items for the order
3. Verify order number and items
4. Find customer (use name/email)
5. Hand-deliver to customer
6. Click "Mark Served ✓" to complete
```

#### **Section 2: 👨‍🍳 Being Prepared** ← Just Watch
```
- Kitchen is working on this order
- Nothing for you to do yet
- Check back when it moves to "Ready for Pickup"
```

#### **Section 3: ⏳ Awaiting Supervisor Approval** ← FYI Only
```
- New online orders waiting for supervisor approval
- You CANNOT approve these - only supervisor can
- Just shows progress - don't worry about it
- Will move to kitchen once supervisor approves
```

### Quick Reference
- **🎯 Ready** = Click "Mark Served ✓"
- **👨‍🍳 Preparing** = Just watch, check back soon
- **⏳ Awaiting** = Nothing to do, supervisor is reviewing

### Customer Service
- Greet online customers same as table customers
- Mention special instructions ("no onions, got it!")
- Offer napkins, sauces, extra utensils
- Deliver quickly - customers are waiting

## 📊 How Orders Are Tracked

### Customer Timeline:
```
1. Customer scans QR → Sees menu
2. Selects items → Adds to cart  
3. Enters name & email → Checks out
4. Order confirmed → Kitchen notified
5. Kitchen prepares → Status updates
6. Order ready → Waiter notified
7. Customer notified via email
8. Customer picks up → Satisfied! ✓
```

### Behind the Scenes:
- **Pending** → Just received, needs kitchen acknowledgment
- **Preparing** → Kitchen is actively working
- **Ready** → Done, waiting for customer
- **Served** → Delivered to customer

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Managers:
- Update QR code during slow periods, not rush hours
- Share link consistently across all platforms
- Track which social media drives most orders
- Monitor customer email for feedback
- Offer small discount for online orders initially

### For Kitchen:
- Keep track of online order count vs dine-in
- Flag rush periods to management
- Communicate estimated times if slammed
- Double-check special instructions
- Keep dedicated prep area for online orders

### For Supervisors:
- Review online order data daily
- Compare online vs table order revenue
- Identify popular menu items for online
- Set up email templates for common messages
- Plan staff based on online order volume

### For Waiters:
- Smile when delivering online orders
- Thank customer for choosing online
- Mention it's faster than waiting in line
- Suggest popular items for next visit
- Create personal touch to build loyalty

## 🆘 FAQ & Troubleshooting

**Q: Customer says QR code doesn't work**
A: 
- Check QR code isn't damaged/printed poorly
- Try short link instead: [your-short-link]
- Regenerate code if very old
- Verify customer has internet connection

**Q: How do customers know order is ready?**
A:
- Email notification is sent automatically
- SMS integration coming soon
- They can check status page with order number

**Q: Can we see order history?**
A:
- Yes, all online orders in supervisor dashboard
- Filter by date, customer, status
- Export for accounting/analytics

**Q: What if order is cancelled?**
A:
- Refund processed immediately
- Customer notified via email
- Order removed from kitchen queue

**Q: How do we handle special requests?**
A:
- Customer enters in special instructions
- Kitchen sees it on order
- Prepare accordingly
- Confirm with waiter before delivery
